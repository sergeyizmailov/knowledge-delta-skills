# Photo subjects by vertical

Literal and occupational — the work done or object handled, never a metaphor or abstract texture. Search these terms directly and download.

| vertical | hero band | content bands |
|---|---|---|
| Legal / licensing / formation | glass towers shot upward, city skyline | desk with pen and documents, handshake, meeting across a table |
| Fintech / brokerage | wall of trading charts, hand holding a phone with the product UI | laptop dashboard, card terminal |
| Accounting / audit | spreadsheets and a calculator | folders on a shelf, someone at a monitor |
| Immigration / relocation | airport terminal, passport and boarding pass | family with luggage, city street |
| Logistics / freight | container port, truck on a motorway | warehouse racking, forklift, pallets |
| Refrigeration / HVAC | rooftop condensers, cold-store interior | technician with a gauge manifold, pipework |
| Construction / engineering | crane against sky, site at dusk | worker with drawings, steel frame |
| Medical / clinic | clinic corridor, building exterior | equipment, clinician with a patient |
| Manufacturing | production line, CNC machine | operator at a control panel, finished parts |
| Security / facilities | control room with monitors | guard at a gate, camera on a wall |
| **Retail / trade counter** | the product in use on a real job, or the trade counter / warehouse exterior | shelf and aisle shots, stock on racking, a hand holding the tool. Product shots themselves are catalogue assets, not photography — see `archetypes/ecommerce.md` |
| **News** | none — the lead story's own photograph | editorial images supplied per article; never stock |
| **Institutional** | the building, or people using the service | departments at work, equipment, a service desk |
| **Directory** | usually none | listings carry a small supplied logo at most |

Holds across verticals: hero photo wide and mid-tone so a flat fill still leaves the subject readable; split-block photos close-range and cluttered, not clean product shots.
