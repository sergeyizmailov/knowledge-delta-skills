# Playbook — Nutra (weight-loss / skin / health, incl. COD)

Reviewed 2026-08-28. Vendor benchmarks below are directional priors, not live data — replace once you have your own.

**Funnel/event:** FB/IG ad → pre-lander (advertorial/quiz) → offer page. Lead = CPA network conversion; COD = order confirmed by call center (real payout gate). Primary KPI = **confirmed CPA** (ad spend ÷ confirmed orders), NOT submitted-lead CPL. Margin/order = payout − confirmed CPA. Confirm lags order by hours–days — judge on confirmed cohort, not same-day submits.

**Creative constraints:** advertorial ("doctor/local-news" story), quiz ("answer 3 questions"); before/after imagery avoided **in-ad** (policy) — transformation lives on the LP instead. Personal Attributes: copy can't imply reader's health/condition ("Struggling with your weight?") — reframe to product benefit. 2–3 pre-lander angles × 1 broad adset each, 1-3-1 screening (directional, not causal — `04`). Spy: AdSpy/Anstrex.

**Review traps / what kills the account:**
- Unsafe supplements outright prohibited (anabolic steroids, chitosan, comfrey, DHEA, ephedra, HGH...). Rx drugs need licensing evidence.
- Before/after under Health & Wellness: non-product weight-loss side-by-sides and idealized transformations prohibited; no negative-self-perception content. Exact cosmetic before/after wording = partially verified, confirm live.
- Pre-lander claim too strong → account/domain death.
- Optimizing to submitted leads while confirm silently drops → CPA looks great, P&L bleeds. Always reconcile to confirmed.

**GEO/device:** COD GEOs (LATAM, EU-t2, SEA) behave differently from straight-sale T1 — call-center + native-operator language quality moves confirm rate more than creative does. Mobile-first LPs, heavy pre-lander. T3 confirm consistently below T1/T2 (qualitative, no clean aggregate). Cheap-CPL GEO at 15% confirm loses to pricier GEO at 45%.

**Economics (vendor bands, 2026-08 — replace with live data):**
| Metric | Range |
|---|---|
| Meta CPM (Health&Wellness proxy; cloaked nutra ≥ this) | ~$20.70 T1 |
| LP→lead CR (general, not nutra-specific) | ~2–5% |
| COD confirm — EU | up to ~50% |
| COD confirm — LATAM | up to ~35% |
| COD confirm — industry avg | 25–40% (50–75% well-optimised) |
| COD confirm — call-center quality | 15–18% poor → 20–25% native operators |
| Payout/confirmed order | ~$17–21 typical; high-ticket to ~$85; GEO range $0.10–$120 |

Confirm rate is the hidden multiplier of real CPA: $17 payout at 25% confirm needs lead CPL ≤ ~$4.25 to break even.

**Metrics discipline:** "lead" = the CPA network's CONFIRMED event per the team's own definition — pin down exactly which tracker status before any CPL math. Confirm arrives on a lag → cohort by click date.
